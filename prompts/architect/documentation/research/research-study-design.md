# User Research Study Design

You are tasked with designing a comprehensive user research study.

## Research Objectives

Define clear research questions:
1. What do we need to learn?
2. Who are our target users?
3. What decisions will this research inform?

## Research Methodology

### Qualitative Methods
- **User Interviews**: One-on-one discussions (30-60 min)
- **Focus Groups**: Group discussions (60-90 min)
- **Contextual Inquiry**: Observing users in their environment
- **Usability Testing**: Task-based testing with think-aloud protocol

### Quantitative Methods
- **Surveys**: Structured questionnaires
- **Analytics Analysis**: Usage data and metrics
- **A/B Testing**: Comparative testing
- **Card Sorting**: Information architecture validation

## Study Design

### 1. Participant Recruitment
- **Target Personas**: Define 2-3 key user types
- **Sample Size**: Minimum participants per method
- **Screening Criteria**: Inclusion/exclusion requirements
- **Recruitment Channels**: Where to find participants

### 2. Research Protocol

**Interview Script Template**:
```
Introduction (5 min)
- Welcome and consent
- Study purpose
- Recording permission

Warm-up (5 min)
- Background questions
- Establish rapport

Main Questions (30 min)
- Open-ended exploration
- Follow-up probes
- Scenario-based questions

Wrap-up (5 min)
- Additional thoughts
- Thank you and next steps
```

### 3. Data Collection
- Recording method (audio, video, notes)
- Consent forms and privacy
- Data storage and security
- Backup procedures

### 4. Analysis Plan
- Thematic analysis approach
- Coding framework
- Synthesis methods
- Reporting format

## Deliverables

1. **Research Plan Document**
   - Objectives and research questions
   - Methodology and timeline
   - Participant criteria
   - Budget and resources

2. **Research Materials**
   - Screener questionnaire
   - Interview guide
   - Consent forms
   - Task scenarios (for usability testing)

3. **Analysis Framework**
   - Coding scheme
   - Data organization template
   - Reporting outline

## Timeline

| Phase | Duration | Activities |
|-------|----------|------------|
| Planning | 1 week | Finalize objectives, create materials |
| Recruitment | 2 weeks | Screen and schedule participants |
| Data Collection | 2-3 weeks | Conduct sessions |
| Analysis | 1-2 weeks | Code and synthesize findings |
| Reporting | 1 week | Create report and presentation |

## Best Practices

1. **Pilot Test**: Run 1-2 practice sessions
2. **Iterative Refinement**: Adjust protocol based on early sessions
3. **Multiple Researchers**: Have 2 people for note-taking
4. **Immediate Debriefs**: Document observations right after sessions
5. **Ethical Considerations**: Protect participant privacy and well-being
