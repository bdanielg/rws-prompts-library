# Interview Question Design

You are tasked with designing effective user interview questions.

## Question Design Principles

### 1. Open-Ended Questions
**Good**:
- "Tell me about the last time you used [product]..."
- "Walk me through your process for..."
- "What challenges do you face when..."

**Avoid**:
- "Do you like this feature?" (Yes/No)
- "Would you use this?" (Hypothetical)
- Leading questions that suggest an answer

### 2. Behavioral vs. Hypothetical

**Behavioral (Preferred)**:
- "Describe a recent situation where..."
- "What did you do the last time..."
- "How have you handled..."

**Hypothetical (Use Sparingly)**:
- "What would you do if..."
- "How might you..."

### 3. Context Gathering

**Background Questions**:
```
- What is your role/background?
- How long have you been doing [task]?
- What tools do you currently use?
- How often do you [perform action]?
```

**Workflow Questions**:
```
- Walk me through your typical [workflow]
- What's the first thing you do when...
- What happens next?
- How do you know when you're done?
```

### 4. Pain Point Discovery

**Effective Probes**:
- "What's the most frustrating part of..."
- "Where do you spend the most time?"
- "What workarounds have you created?"
- "If you could change one thing..."

**Follow-ups**:
- "Can you tell me more about that?"
- "Why is that important?"
- "How often does that happen?"
- "What's the impact when that occurs?"

## Interview Structure

### Introduction (5 min)
```
1. Welcome and thank you
2. Explain study purpose
3. Consent and recording permission
4. Set expectations (no right answers)
5. Q&A about the process
```

### Warm-up (5 min)
```
- Tell me about your role
- How long have you been [doing X]?
- What does a typical day look like?
```

### Core Questions (30-40 min)

**Topic 1: Current State**
```
- How do you currently [accomplish task]?
- What tools do you use?
- Walk me through a recent example
```

**Topic 2: Pain Points**
```
- What's challenging about [process]?
- Where do things go wrong?
- What takes the most time?
```

**Topic 3: Needs & Goals**
```
- What would make [task] easier?
- What's missing in current solutions?
- What would success look like?
```

### Wrap-up (5 min)
```
- Is there anything we didn't cover?
- Any questions for me?
- Thank you and next steps
```

## Question Bank by Topic

### **Product Discovery**
1. What problem were you trying to solve when you first started using [product]?
2. How did you find out about [product]?
3. What made you decide to try it?
4. What alternatives did you consider?

### **Feature Usage**
1. Show me how you use [feature]
2. When was the last time you used this?
3. What triggers you to use this feature?
4. What do you expect to happen when you [action]?

### **Workflow & Context**
1. Walk me through your process for [task]
2. What information do you need to get started?
3. Who else is involved in this process?
4. What happens before/after this step?

### **Pain Points**
1. What's the hardest part of [task]?
2. When does this process break down?
3. What workarounds have you created?
4. If you had a magic wand, what would you change?

### **Goals & Priorities**
1. What are you trying to accomplish?
2. How do you measure success?
3. What's most important to you?
4. What would make this faster/easier/better?

## Interviewing Techniques

### Active Listening
- Let participants finish thoughts
- Use silence to encourage elaboration
- Take notes but maintain eye contact
- Reflect back what you heard

### Probing Techniques
```
"Tell me more about that..."
"Can you give me an example?"
"Why is that important?"
"How did that make you feel?"
"What happened next?"
```

### Handling Challenges

**Participant Goes Off-Topic**:
- Acknowledge their point
- Gently redirect: "That's interesting. Going back to..."

**Short Answers**:
- Use silence (count to 5)
- Ask for examples or stories
- Probe with "why" or "how"

**Vague Responses**:
- "Can you be more specific?"
- "Can you show me?"
- "Walk me through that step-by-step"

## Best Practices

1. **One Question at a Time**: Avoid compound questions
2. **Avoid Jargon**: Use participant's language
3. **Stay Neutral**: Don't show bias or judgment
4. **Dig Deeper**: Ask "why" at least 5 times
5. **Concrete Examples**: Request specific stories
6. **Observe Behavior**: Note what they do vs. what they say
